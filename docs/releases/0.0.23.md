# NiceWP 0.0.23 — Public Release & Stabilization

> **Welcome to the first official public release of NiceWallpaper (NiceWP)!**

NiceWP 0.0.23 brings together months of engineering to deliver a modern, resource-conscious, and privacy-respecting wallpaper engine for Windows 10 and 11.

---

## 🌟 Major Highlights

### 1. Seamless Desktop Integration
- **WorkerW Architecture**: Renders directly behind your desktop icons using Windows native DirectComposition and hardware-accelerated video pipelines.
- **Dual-Process Reliability**: Front-end UI (`NiceWP.App`) and presentation host (`NiceWP.Runtime.Host`) run in isolated processes connected via high-speed named pipes. Closing the management app leaves your wallpaper running cleanly.

### 2. Adaptive Desktop Intelligence & Smart Scenes
- **Context-Aware Wallpapers**: Evaluates battery levels, active applications, and time of day to select the perfect wallpaper for your current focus (Focus, Gaming, Relax, Night, Battery Saver).
- **Zero-Footprint Gaming**: Automatically pauses video rendering during fullscreen games or movies, returning 100% of GPU and CPU resources to your application.

### 3. Interactive Page Turn
- **Corner Drag Gesture**: Drag from the bottom-right corner of your desktop to curl the active wallpaper in real time at 60 FPS, previewing the next item before committing or releasing.

### 4. Discover / Online Library
- **Integrated Open-License Search**: Discover thousands of high-resolution wallpapers directly from Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, and NASA Image and Video Library.
- **Safety First**: Full SSRF protection, HTTPS enforcement, and permanent local provenance retention (license terms, author attribution, and source links).

### 5. Local Personalization Engine
- **Taste Learning Without the Cloud**: Teach NiceWP what you love ("More Like This", "Less Like This", "Never Show Again").
- **100% Private & Resettable**: All calculations run locally on your machine with zero telemetry, zero accounts, and zero cloud profiling.

---

## 💾 Installation

### Option 1: Standard Installer (Recommended)
Download and execute `NiceWP-0.0.23-win-x64-Setup.exe`.
- Installs to `%LocalAppData%\Programs\NiceWP` (no UAC elevation required).
- Includes automatic Start Menu and Desktop shortcuts.

### Option 2: Portable ZIP
Download `NiceWP-0.0.23-win-x64.zip`, extract to any folder, and run `NiceWP.App.exe`.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security Guarantees

- **Zero Telemetry**: No phone-home tracking, no analytics, no identifiers.
- **Deterministic Storage**: Everything lives in `%LocalAppData%\NiceWP\` and can be inspected or wiped at any time.
- **Safe Downloads**: Strict input validation, size limits, and magic byte checking.

---

## ⚠️ Known Limitations

- **Multi-Monitor**: Currently displays on the primary display. Independent multi-monitor assignment is slated for a future milestone.
- **Hardware Variation**: Very low-end integrated graphics processors may experience occasional micro-stutters during high-speed corner page curl gestures.
- **Online Availability**: Search response times in the Discover tab depend on third-party provider server availability.
